Threading guide

Work through the steps in order. Each step lists only the crossings with rope that is already down when you get there; the rest show up later, under the step that lays that rope.

    The mat path uses the Lissajous approach described by Nils Kristian Rossing in How to Make Rope Mats and Rosettes. Free to use for your own mats.
